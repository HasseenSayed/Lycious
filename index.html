Made with Pure Attention

At The Lycious Company, we believe that a great drink starts with a great fruit. We searched for a lychee drink that truly captured the natural, exotic sweetness of fresh lychee — and couldn't find one. So we decided to make it ourselves.

"Made to pause for a moment. To treat yourself. Pure attention."

Every bottle of Lycious is crafted with real lychee puree, carefully balanced to be subtly sweet, refreshingly crisp, and absolutely delicious. No artificial colors — just natural goodness in a beautiful 275ml glass bottle.
