# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Lycious serves both consumers discovering the drink and hospitality or retail buyers evaluating it for their assortment. The website should help both audiences understand the product and brand before directing them toward ordering or stockist information.

## Product Purpose

Lycious is the digital brand presence for a premium lychee drink. Its primary purpose is brand discovery: helping visitors understand the drink, its taste, its ingredients, its story, and where it can be found. Order requests and stockist discovery support that goal rather than replacing it.

## Positioning

Lycious presents a carefully made lychee drink that aims to do justice to the fruit's real taste. It is made with 5% real lychee puree, balanced to be subtly sweet and refreshing, and locally produced in Heemskerk.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the website in Dutch or English, learn about the brand and bottle, inspect product and nutrition information, find stockists, and may submit an order request or contact the company. Hospitality and retail buyers use the same brand experience to evaluate Lycious for their venue or assortment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The product is sold in a 275 ml glass bottle.
- It contains 5% real lychee puree and has 29 kcal per 100 ml.
- The glass packaging is recyclable.
- The website supports Dutch and English.
- Brand discovery and presence are the primary website goals.
- Stockist, contact, and order-request paths must remain clear secondary actions.
- Product, nutrition, availability, or sustainability claims must not be invented or strengthened beyond evidence on hand.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Lycious name and existing logo assets. Preserve the established product facts, bilingual experience, and the brand's attentive, premium, fruit-led voice.

## Evidence on Hand

- Lycious logo assets in `images/Logos/`.
- Product and bottle imagery in `images/` and a bottle model in `models/`.
- Product, nutrition, stockist, contact, and order-request content in `index.html` and `script.js`.
- Confirmed facts: 275 ml bottle, 5% real lychee puree, 29 kcal per 100 ml, locally produced in Heemskerk, and recyclable glass packaging.
- No confirmed testimonials, customer logos, awards, benchmarks, or press claims are currently on hand; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with memorable brand and product discovery.
2. Make the drink's real, confirmed qualities easy to understand.
3. Serve consumers and trade buyers without splitting the brand into competing experiences.
4. Keep ordering, stockist discovery, and contact paths clear but secondary to the story.
5. Treat factual product claims with precision and restraint.
